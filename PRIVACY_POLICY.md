# Privacy Policy for xScholr

**Effective Date:** May 09, 2026  
**Owner:** Jabiru Labs

### 1. Introduction
xScholr is a social platform dedicated to sharing and discovering scholarship opportunities. This Privacy Policy describes how Jabiru Labs ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application.

### 2. Information We Collect
We collect information you provide directly to us through the following app features:
*   **Account Registration:** Email address, password, full name, and a unique username.
*   **Google Authentication:** If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your Google ID, email, and profile picture URL.
*   **User Profile:** Information you voluntarily add, including your **GPA**, **Education History**, **Work Experience**, and **Biography**.
*   **User-Generated Content:** Scholarship posts (including titles, descriptions, and external links), comments on posts, and likes.
*   **Media:** Images you upload when sharing scholarship opportunities (processed via your device’s gallery or camera).

### 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data to:
*   **Facilitate the Social Feed:** Display your scholarship posts and comments to other users.
*   **Verification System:** Monitor posts for our "Verified" and "Under Review" status to ensure community safety.
*   **Notifications:** Send alerts about new scholarship opportunities, followers, and comments.
*   **Personalization:** Show you relevant educational content based on your profile and interactions.

### 4. Data Storage and Security
*   **Backend Hosting:** Your data is securely stored on our backend servers (hosted via Railway).
*   **Authentication:** We use industry-standard encryption for passwords and secure tokens (JWT) for session management.
*   **Images:** Media files are stored using cloud storage services (Firebase Storage).

### 5. Sharing of Information
*   **Public Visibility:** By design, your profile (except email) and your posts are visible to other registered users of xScholr.
*   **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

### 6. Your Rights and Controls
*   **Edit/Delete Content:** You have the right to edit or delete any scholarship post you have created.
*   **Profile Management:** You can update your GPA, Education, and Experience at any time.
*   **Account Deletion:** You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting our support team.

### 7. Third-Party Services
We integrate with the following services:
*   **Google Sign-In:** Governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
*   **Firebase Analytics:** Used to understand app performance and crashes.

### 8. Children's Privacy
xScholr is intended for students and academic professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it immediately.
